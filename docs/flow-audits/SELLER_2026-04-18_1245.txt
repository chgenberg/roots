CHILD SAFETY / GDPR RED FLAGS
------------------------------
- **No age gate or parental-consent flow** on `POST /register/seller` or `registrera/saljare/[token]` — only invite token, email, password, displayName, optional phone (`auth.ts`, signup page).
- **No parent/guardian role or linked guardian account** in `user_role` — cannot offer supervised/minor-first UX (`packages/db` users schema).
- **Public shop and API** expose `displayName`, team/org context, aggregates; **slug derived from name** increases identifiability; no `publicAlias` / opt-out from internal ranking (`shop.ts`, `shop/[slug]/page.tsx`, `sellers` schema).
- **Team leaderboard** (authenticated coach/org surfaces) orders sellers by sales with **real names** — no per-seller hide/opt-out flag.
- **Lagansvarig-created seller accounts with leader-chosen passwords** — consent/credential control issues for youth (`dashboard.ts` POST sellers, `lag/saljare/page.tsx`).
- **Silent session switch** after seller self-register if another user was logged in — family/shared-device confusion & account integrity (`auth.ts` createSession after signup).

FIRST-SALE ACCELERATORS
------------------------
1. **Sticky primary “Dela min shop”** on `min-shop` (mirror fixed bottom bar pattern used on `shop/[slug]` checkout) — reduces scroll-to-share on phones.
2. **`getByInviteToken` (or public invite preview)** on landing — show org/team/campaign **before** commit; builds trust and reduces wrong-signup anxiety.
3. **Fix `first_sale` milestone “Nästa” logic** — special-case order-count vs öre in `getNextMilestone` / achievement alignment (`milestones.ts`).
4. **Wire `GET /v1/sharing/shop-share-template`** into min-shop — short SMS-tuned copy vs full `campaign.story` in `navigator.share`.
5. **Server-side metadata for `/shop/[slug]`** — dynamic title/description (and future image) so shared links preview correctly in WhatsApp/iMessage.
