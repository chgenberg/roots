SAFETY / TONE RED FLAGS
------------------------
• **Portal & sales UI copy** promises pipeline, quotes, KPIs, admin/system access while the backend serves the **public visitor prompt** — **false-confidence / invented CRM answers** (P1).
• **`PUBLIC_CHAT_SYSTEM_PROMPT`** embeds **fixed prices, shipping, and consumer-law style facts** — **policy & pricing drift** vs live `/villkor` and CMS (P1).
• **No earnings / outcome guardrails** in chat prompts — risky for youth sellers asking “how much can I make?” (P1).
• **`SYSTEM_PROMPT`** (authenticated `/chat` route) **omits** the explicit **no medical advice** line present in the public prompt — **regression risk** if `/v1/ai/chat` is wired later (P1/P2).
• **`/v1/ai/chat`** accepts **`history` without stripping `system`** — **prompt injection / instruction override** for authenticated callers (P1).
• **Cosmetic efficacy language** (microbiome, antibakteriellt, etc.) adjacent to “no medical advice” — **claims-adjacent** blur (P2).
• **Mixed support emails** (`support@roots.se` vs `hej@roots.se`) across prompts and fallbacks — **trust / ops** inconsistency (P2).
