Verdict: Marketing product pages render cleanly as a static brand catalog, but they are **not wired to the commerce source of truth** and **misroute B2B buyers** who arrive from `/portal/produkter`. Prices happen to match DB seed values today (149/129/399 kr), yet names, SKUs, images, and copy are duplicated in four places and already diverge from `products` table + portal fallbacks. No add-to-cart, no related products, no reviews. JSON-LD has schema correctness gaps (relative image, invented SKUs). Detail mobile sticky CTA can obscure INCI block.

Top quick wins:
1. Single shared product catalog module fed by DB/API (or at minimum one TS constant imported everywhere).
2. Role-aware CTA on PDP: logged-in CLUB_MEMBER/CLUB_ADMIN → `/portal/produkter` or deep-link add-to-cart; PUBLIC → `/foreningsliv`.
3. Fix ProductJsonLd: absolute image URL, real SKU from DB, optional `metadataBase`-resolved `url`.
4. Add `pb-24` (or safe-area) on PDP so sticky bar does not cover ingredients.
5. “Andra produkter” row on PDP linking sibling slugs + bundle upsell (399 kr).

=====================================================
PRIORITY 1 — MVP-BLOCKING
=====================================================

[1.1] **B2B portal links to marketing PDP with wrong checkout CTA** — `/portal/produkter` links each card to `publicProductHref(slug)` → `/produkter/[slug]`, but PDP primary + sticky CTAs always say “Beställ via din förening” and route to `/foreningsliv` (association signup/marketing), **not** back to portal cart or `/portal/bestallningar`. CLUB_MEMBER flow breaks at the moment of purchase intent.
Files: apps/web/src/app/(portal)/portal/produkter/page.tsx (~104–125, ~179)
        apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/[slug]/page.tsx (~165–167, ~193–195)
Detail: Detect session/role or `?from=portal` and show “Lägg i beställning” → portal cart; keep föreningsliv CTA for anonymous PUBLIC only.

[1.2] **No “Lägg i kundvagn” / add-to-cart on marketing product surfaces** — listing and detail expose price + “Läs mer” / föreningsliv CTAs only. There is zero path from `/produkter` to supporter shop (`/shop/[slug]`) or B2B order dialog. For MVP commerce story, product pages are **browse-only dead ends** unless user self-navigates to portal or a seller shop.
Files: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/page.tsx (~117–124, ~137–141)
        apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/[slug]/page.tsx (~163–167)
Detail: At minimum, secondary CTA for CLUB_MEMBER (“Beställ i portalen”) and clarify PUBLIC path (föreningsliv vs find seller shop).

[1.3] **Product catalog hardcoded in marketing — not DB/API source of truth** — Full product records (name, slug, price string, image, INCI, copy) live in local `PRODUCTS` constants on listing + detail pages. DB seed (`packages/db/src/seed.ts`) uses different **display names** (“Roots Shampoo” vs “First Growth”), English descriptions, and portal mapping (`portal-products.ts`) uses different **hero images** for shampoo (`p1.jpg` vs marketing `m3.jpg`). Price integers match today (14900/12900/39900 öre) but will drift silently on any DB/catalog update.
Files: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/page.tsx (~15–46)
        apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/[slug]/page.tsx (~13–84, ~106)
        packages/db/src/seed.ts (~112–139)
        apps/web/src/lib/portal-products.ts (~1–54)
        apps/web/src/components/sections/products-preview.tsx (~7–32) [adjacent duplicate]
Detail: Fetch active products server-side (tRPC or `/v1/portal/products` public variant) or import one shared catalog; format prices from `priceOre` via shared `formatSek`.

[1.4] **ProductJsonLd SKU values do not match database SKUs** — Detail page builds `sku={\`ROOTS-${slug.toUpperCase().replace("-", "")}-001\`}` producing e.g. `ROOTS-SHAMPOO-001`, `ROOTS-BODYWASH-001`. Seeded SKUs are `ROOTS-SH-001`, `ROOTS-CO-001`, `ROOTS-BW-001`. Structured data, ERP, and order lines will not reconcile.
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/[slug]/page.tsx (~110–116)
        packages/db/src/seed.ts (~114–137)
Detail: Pass real `sku` from catalog/DB row into ProductJsonLd.

[1.5] **ProductJsonLd `image` is a relative path** — Passes `/images/m3.jpg` etc. Google Rich Results / Schema validators expect absolute URLs; PUBLIC audit [2.11] still applies. `metadataBase` exists in root layout but is not used to resolve product image in JSON-LD.
Files: apps/web/src/components/json-ld.tsx (~73–74)
        apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/[slug]/page.tsx (~115)
Detail: `new URL(product.image, metadataBase).toString()` (or `${SITE_URL}${product.image}`).

=====================================================
ROLE CONNECTIONS
=====================================================

PUBLIC (logged-out visitor)
  - `/produkter` and `/produkter/[slug]` are the intended discovery layer: images, prices, INCI, brand story.
  - CTAs correctly push toward `/foreningsliv` for association acquisition, but there is **no D2C path** to a supporter shop from product pages (must know a seller URL).
  - JSON-LD helps SEO; fixes [1.4–1.5] matter for organic product rich results.
  - No reviews/trust layer hurts conversion (CONVERSION_TRUST).

CLUB_MEMBER (B2B buyer, `/portal`)
  - Portal catalog links **into** marketing PDP for product detail — then **strands** user with föreningsliv CTA [1.1]. Critical MVP break.
  - Separate issue (out of file scope but blocks MVP): portal cart on `/portal/produkter` does not persist to `/portal/bestallningar` (CLUB_MEMBER audit [1.5]).
  - Marketing prices align with DB wholesale list price; no volume tiers shown on marketing pages.

CLUB_ADMIN
  - Same as CLUB_MEMBER for catalog browsing; association onboarding not linked from product pages (uses `/foreningsliv`).
  - Product pages do not explain club margin, campaign pricing, or MOQ — expectation set only on foreningsliv/portal.

ASSOCIATION_ADMIN
  - Product pages support **pre-sales storytelling** for admins evaluating sortiment before `/registrera`.
  - No link from PDP to association dashboard, campaign setup, or “starta kampanj”.
  - Bundle 399 kr messaging matches seed bundle but routes to marketing funnel, not association tooling.

Supporter shop (adjacent, not in scope)
  - `/shop/[slug]` has real cart (`useCart`) and öre-based prices from API; marketing `/produkter` is a **parallel catalog** — intentional split but confusing if prices/names diverge.

Overall MVP readiness for **product pages as commerce entrypoints**: **NOT READY** — acceptable as static marketing brochure only. **Ready for MVP** only after [1.1–1.3] (catalog single-source + B2B CTA routing) and JSON-LD fixes [1.4–1.5].

Recommended MVP gate (this scout):
  □ Shared catalog from DB or one module
  □ PDP CTA routes CLUB_MEMBER → portal order flow
  □ ProductJsonLd SKU + absolute image
  □ Mobile PDP bottom inset for sticky CTA
  □ Related products + bundle cross-link
