NOTE ON ROUTES
--------------
Scout brief lists `/portal/*` TL pages — **those routes do not exist for team leaders**.
`portal/lag/` and `portal/bjud-in/` are absent. Login redirects `TEAM_LEADER` → `/lag`
(`login/page.tsx:47-48`), not `/portal`. The live coach experience is the fundraising shell:
`/lag`, `/lag/saljare`, `/lag/bestallningar`, `/lag/avrakning` (`fundraising/layout.tsx:98-104`).

`portal/page.tsx` has branches for CLUB, SALES, and a default **AdminDashboard** only — no
`TEAM_LEADER` case (`portal/page.tsx:614-620`). `portal/saljare/page.tsx` is an **internal
admin / sales-rep** pipeline table (`GET /portal/sellers`), not the team roster.

SUMMARY
-------
The team-leader **fundraising shell** (`/lag/*`) is the real product surface and covers core
coach jobs: team KPIs, seller ranking, invite URL copy, inline seller create, per-seller goals,
pause/reactivate, and orders/CSV. MVP blockers remain: broken onboarding when no team row exists,
misleading KPI math (all orders vs PAID revenue), pause-seller not enforced at checkout/shop,
coach-chosen seller passwords, and invite distribution limited to raw URL (no SMS template, QR,
share-sheet, or token rotation UI). Portal paths in scope are stubs or wrong-role shells.
Findings: 24 (P1: 6, P2: 9, P3: 6, P4: 3)
MVP readiness: 54/100

[1.1] Order KPI vs revenue mismatch on team overview
  File: apps/web/src/app/(fundraising)/lag/page.tsx:156-157,112
