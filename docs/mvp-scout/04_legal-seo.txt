Legal pages (köpvillkor + integritetspolicy) are substantially complete for a Swedish B2C launch: ångerrätt, reklamation, GDPR rights, ARN, EU ODR, IMY, and contact are all present. Since the April 2026 conversion/trust audit, LegalIdentity has been centralised and is now rendered in footer, kontakt, villkor, integritet, and shop/kassa footer — a major gap closed. Remaining MVP blockers are (1) unverified/placeholder company constants (phone, possibly address), (2) missing GDPR disclosure for AI håranalys automated processing, and (3) structured-data/SEO polish gaps that hurt discoverability but not checkout legality.

Compared to docs/flow-audits/CONVERSION_TRUST_2026-04-18_1313.txt and MASTER_PLAN Fas 4: footer/legal/JSON-LD items from that audit are largely implemented. Canonical URLs, per-page OG, contactPoint in JSON-LD, and env-driven legal constants remain open.

=====================================================
PRIORITY 1 — MVP-BLOCKING
=====================================================

[P1.1] **Placeholder telephone in published legal identity**
File: apps/web/src/lib/legal-identity.ts (contact.phone: "+46 8 000 000 00")
Also emitted in: apps/web/src/components/json-ld.tsx (OrganizationJsonLd → telephone)
Impact: Fake phone in schema.org Organization misleads consumers and search engines; violates e-handelslagen expectation of reachable seller contact. LegalIdentityBlock does not surface phone in UI, but JSON-LD does.
Fix: Replace with verified business phone before launch; add phone to LegalIdentityBlock when showContact=true, or remove telephone from JSON-LD until real.

[P1.2] **Registered address / org.nr not verifiable from repo**
File: apps/web/src/lib/legal-identity.ts
Values: orgNumber "559517-3210", vatId "SE559517321001", street "Storgatan 1", 111 51 Stockholm
Impact: Format is structurally valid (559-prefix AB, VAT = SE + org digits + 01), but "Storgatan 1" reads as generic placeholder and appears nowhere else in the monorepo. If these do not match Bolagsverket/Fortnox records, every footer, legal page, and JSON-LD block publishes false trader identity — a hard launch blocker under e-handelslagen and EU consumer law.
Fix: Confirm against company registration; update legal-identity.ts to exact registered postadress. Consider Bolagsverket link or " säte" wording if virtual office.

[P1.3] **Integritetspolicy lacks AI / automated-decision disclosure for håranalys**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/integritet/page.tsx
Gap: Section 2 mentions håranalys images + OpenAI, but no Art. 13/22 language on automated profiling, logic involved, significance/consequences, or right to human review — required when offering GPT Vision recommendations that influence product suggestions.
Impact: GDPR compliance gap for a live lead magnet promoted in sitemap (/haranalys) and marketing.
Fix: Add subsection under håranalys or "Dina rättigheter" covering automated analysis, opt-out/human contact path, and that recommendations are advisory not binding.

=====================================================
PRIORITY 2 — HIGH
=====================================================

[P2.1] **No standard withdrawal form (ångerrättsformulär) linked from köpvillkor**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/villkor/page.tsx (§4 Ångerrätt)
Gap: Email contact is provided, but distansavtalslagen expects access to a model withdrawal form (often EU standard template PDF or dedicated page).
Fix: Add /villkor/angerratt or PDF link; reference it in §4.

[P2.2] **ProductJsonLd uses relative image URL**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/produkter/[slug]/page.tsx → ProductJsonLd image="/images/m3.jpg" etc.
Impact: Google Rich Results prefer absolute URLs; may suppress product rich snippets.
Fix: Prefix with metadataBase / NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL (as MASTER_PLAN Fas 4 notes).

[P2.3] **Canonical URLs missing on almost all marketing pages**
Files: villkor, integritet, produkter, produkter/[slug], foreningsliv, om-oss, kontakt, haranalys, homepage
Only shop layout sets alternates.canonical (apps/web/src/app/(shop)/shop/[slug]/layout.tsx).
Impact: Duplicate-URL risk if query params or trailing slashes proliferate; weaker SEO consolidation.
Fix: Add alternates: { canonical: "/path" } per public route (metadataBase in root layout makes relative paths valid).

[P2.4] **Per-page Open Graph / Twitter incomplete**
Root layout (apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx) sets default OG/Twitter images — good fallback.
Explicit OG: kontakt layout, haranalys layout, shop layout, login layout.
Missing explicit OG/twitter on: villkor, integritet, produkter, produkter/[slug], foreningsliv, om-oss, homepage (inherits root only — no page-specific title/description in OG block).
Impact: Shared links to legal/product pages unfurl generic hero, not page-specific copy.
Fix: Extend metadata on each public marketing page with openGraph + twitter mirroring title/description; use product image on product detail.

[P2.5] **LegalIdentityBlock showContact omits phone**
File: apps/web/src/components/legal-identity-block.tsx
Impact: kontakt page shows address + org.nr but no phone; component design says "phone number" in docblock but only renders email when showContact=true. Inconsistent with JSON-LD telephone field once P1.1 is fixed.
Fix: Render LEGAL_IDENTITY.contact.phone in block variant when showContact.

[P2.6] **Köpvillkor — price change "utan föregående meddelande"**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/villkor/page.tsx (§2)
Impact: Conflicts with checkout UX (price locked at order time) and weakens consumer-trust signal; flagged in CONVERSION_TRUST audit [1.18].
Fix: Reword to "pris vid beställningstillfället gäller" and limit changes to future orders only.

[P2.7] **Integritet — no explicit data controller contact for GDPR requests beyond email**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/integritet/page.tsx (§7)
Gap: Email hej@roots.se given; no postal address repeat in rights section (only in §1 via LegalIdentityBlock). IMY link present — good.
Fix: Repeat postal address in §7 for formal DSR requests; optional DPO line if appointed.

=====================================================
PRIORITY 3 — MEDIUM
=====================================================

[P3.1] **OrganizationJsonLd missing contactPoint array**
File: apps/web/src/components/json-ld.tsx
Gap: MASTER_PLAN Fas 4 requested contactPoint; current schema has flat email/telephone only.
Fix: Add contactPoint with contactType "customer service", availableLanguage "Swedish".

[P3.2] **ProductJsonLd seller object minimal**
File: apps/web/src/components/json-ld.tsx (offers.seller)
Gap: Only legalName; no url, vatID, or address on seller Organization.
Fix: Spread LEGAL_IDENTITY fields into seller for richer merchant markup.

[P3.3] **sitemap.ts omits /hjalp**
File: apps/web/src/app/sitemap.ts
Present: /, /produkter (+3 slugs), /foreningsliv, /om-oss, /kontakt, /haranalys, /integritet, /villkor
Missing: /hjalp (public FAQ hub at apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/hjalp/page.tsx)
Impact: Help content not discoverable via sitemap; page also has no layout metadata.

[P3.4] **/hjalp has no metadata export**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/hjalp/page.tsx ("use client", no layout.tsx)
Impact: Generic title from root template; no description for SEO.
Fix: Add hjalp/layout.tsx with metadata (pattern: kontakt, haranalys).

[P3.5] **robots.ts — no explicit /admin disallow (portal covered)**
File: apps/web/src/app/robots.ts
Current disallow: /api/, /portal/, /forening/, /lag/, /min-shop/, /login, /registrera
Allow: /, /shop/
Assessment: /portal/ correctly blocks all portal routes including audit-log (INTERNAL_ADMIN). No /admin web route exists today. /preview-gate not disallowed but page sets robots: { index: false } — defence in depth gap only.
Fix: Add /preview-gate to disallow list; add /admin/ if route is introduced later.

[P3.6] **Homepage has no page-level metadata file**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/page.tsx (no metadata export)
Impact: Relies entirely on root layout defaults — acceptable but no homepage-specific canonical or OG description beyond root.
Fix: Optional marketing/page metadata or layout with canonical "/".

[P3.7] **Dynamic seller shops not in sitemap**
File: apps/web/src/app/sitemap.ts
Note: /shop/[slug] intentionally omitted (dynamic, unbounded). robots allows /shop/ for viral discovery — correct tradeoff. Consider sitemap index or API-driven sitemap for active campaigns if SEO on seller links becomes priority.

[P3.8] **Integritet §8 cookies vs session cookies**
File: apps/web/src/app/(marketing)/integritet/page.tsx
Text claims "inga tredjepartscookies" and "cookiefri" analytics; also states sessionscookies for login/cart. Accurate if Plausible is cookiefri, but no cookie banner/consent mechanism referenced.
Impact: Likely OK for strictly-necessary session cookies; document Plausible explicitly in §8 if enabled via NEXT_PUBLIC_PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN.

ROLE CONNECTIONS
=====================================================

PUBLIC (visitor / supporter / lead)
  • Trust at first visit: footer + JSON-LD Organization signal legitimacy before any signup.
  • Supporter checkout: shop layout footer + kassa villkor/integritet links satisfy pre-contract disclosure at payment commitment.
  • Håranalys lead magnet: integritet must cover AI processing (P1.3) before scaling traffic via /haranalys SEO.
  • Product discovery: ProductJsonLd + sitemap /produkter paths feed Google Shopping / rich results for cold traffic.

ALL roles
  • Single legal-identity.ts source prevents footer vs email vs Fortnox mismatch.
  • robots blocking /portal, /forening, /lag keeps authenticated surfaces out of search; public /shop/ remains indexable for viral seller links.
  • villkor/integritet linked from footer on every marketing page via Footer component.

Indirect
  • Sales/association onboarding (registrera blocked in robots — correct).
  • Email domain split (rootshaircare.se in API emails) out of scope but still erodes trust if legal pages say roots.se only — coordinate with brand scout.
